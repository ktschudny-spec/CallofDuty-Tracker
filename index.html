Call of Duty Stats Guide

Kills
A kill is counted in this tracker when you eliminate an opponent in a match.
Deaths
A death is counted in the tracker when you get eliminated by an opponent.
Highest Streak
Your highest streak is the biggest amount of kills that you have gotten without dying.

For example, if you have went on a 13 kill streak and that's the highest you have gotten, that'll be your highest streak.

How the Stats Work

This website is for fun. The statistics are randomly generated whenever you choose GENERATE NEW STATS.

The statistics are not and can't be connected to an actual Call of Duty account.

Scorestreak Guide

What Is a Scorestreak?

A scorestreak is a reward that you get when you get a certain amount of score in a match.

In Call of Duty, score can come from eliminations, objectives, assists, and other actions.

Scorestreaks in This Tracker

The Scorestreaks in this tracker are randomly selected by the tracker.

Important Note

Scorestreaks can be different depending on the Call of Duty title.

The scorestreaks on this website are real scorestreaks from different types of Call of Duty games.
